# Hero photos (optional)

The home hero loads three RUET photos from Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0) and falls back to
illustrations if they can't load.

To use your own photos, drop files here named `hero-1`, `hero-2`, `hero-3`, `hero-4`
(`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` or `.webp`). A local photo replaces the slide with the same number and removes
the Wikimedia credit for it. Slide 2 is the study-abroad globe illustration unless you add `hero-2`.

Portrait-ish crops around 1000 x 1200 px work best. Keep files under about 300 KB for fast loading.
Use photos you own or have a licence to use.
